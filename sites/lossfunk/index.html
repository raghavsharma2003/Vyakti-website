Vyakti · relational intelligence

AI knows almost everything. Except you.

Vyakti is research toward AI that understands the person in front of it: what they really understand, how they think, what they feel, and how to earn their trust. It gets better at each person with every conversation. We start with learning, the one place where understanding a person can be measured.

01 · The problem

Intelligence is no longer the bottleneck. Understanding the person is.

Models now write production code, and an AI system has produced a machine-checked proof for Navier–Stokes, a Millennium Prize problem. Yet a tutor still can’t tell whether a child understood, a coach can’t tell when you are about to quit, and an assistant agrees with you when it should push back.

Remembering a person is close to solved. Understanding one, acting on that understanding, and getting better at it over time are not, and almost nobody measures them.

02 · Where we start

Learning is where misreading a person costs the most, and where it can be proven.

  • Confusion hides. In class, a student asks about one question every nine hours. With a one-to-one tutor, about 26 questions get asked an hour. Graesser & Person 1994
  • Learners can’t see it either. How well people judge their own understanding tracks reality at only about .27. Dunlosky & Lipko 2007
  • AI tutors make it worse when they trust surface success. Practice scores rose 48%; exam scores fell 17%. Bastani et al., PNAS 2025
  • And learning has an answer key. A fresh problem a week later shows whether the AI was right about the person.

03 · The broader vision

Understanding a person, mapped.

Learning is the first door. Behind it is the whole person: seven families, 25 dimensions. A to E describe the person; F and G describe the AI, its own self and the loop that makes it better. Each dimension has a core question, a reason it is still open, and a ground truth that could prove the AI right, which decides what gets researched first.

04 · The insight that ties it together

People reveal more to someone they trust. So the relationship is part of the instrument.

.00

How strongly the strength of the relationship predicts outcomes in therapy, across nearly 300 studies and more than 30,000 patients. Teams that feel safe admit errors and ask for help.Flückiger et al. 2018 · Edmondson 1999

For an AI, memory, one steady personality and a bond that grows are not product features. They are how honest signals get in. Relational intelligence and understanding are one loop.

05 · The hypothesis

Language models learned language by predicting the next word. Vyakti learns a person by predicting the person.

It forms beliefs about the whole person: what you knowA, how you learnB, what you feelC, what your talk gives awayD, how far you trust itE. Every belief is a bet on what you will do next, and reality grades every bet. Each miss rewrites the theory, so it should understand you better in week six than in week one, and teach you better because of it.

H1 · SeeingABCDWhat a person knows, feels and hides shows in ordinary interaction

Wrong ifits reading predicts what they do a week later no better than asking them.

H2 · ActingBGWriting a belief before each move turns inference into action

Wrong ifmoves fit the person no better, judged blind.

H3 · TrustingCEFContinuity and a steady self make people reveal more

Wrong ifadmitted confusion doesn’t rise with memory switched on.

H4 · GrowingA–EGAccuracy about each person rises with time, in every family

Wrong ifthe hit rate stays flat, or plain memory keeps pace.

06 · A theory that never sets

Every belief is a bet. None of them is permanent.

Vyakti keeps a written theory of each person, in its own words. Every interaction can move it. Beliefs reality contradicts get rewritten, with the reason kept. Beliefs nobody re-confirms fade. So it can’t harden into a label.

  • Evidence, not impressionsEvery belief cites what it saw.
  • Confidence that movesEach interaction nudges it up or down.
  • Fades without fresh proofOld beliefs decay unless retested.
  • Yours to read and correctThe person can see it, fix it, delete it.

07 · The system

Software made of minds, each on its own clock

LLM-native end to end. No hand-written decision tree: every judgement is a model’s, written down with its reason. Small fast models think inside the pause before the teacher speaks. Strong models reflect overnight. Every week the system rewrites its own methods. Code keeps the records. Reality keeps the score.

08 · Multimodal, both ways

Every signal in. Any surface out.

Understanding rarely sits in the words alone. Vyakti reads what a learner gives it, with consent: voice, words, hands, screen and time. Each signal alone is a hint; together they are evidence. Then the Director chooses the surface the way it chooses words.

09 · Learning anything

Change the subject. Keep the understanding.

The answer key changes from subject to subject. The person doesn’t. What Vyakti learns about how you learn, what you avoid and how you talk travels with you: from fractions to French, from code to chemistry, from a child’s homework to an adult’s new career.

That is how one system could make expert one-to-one teaching, the most effective form of teaching we know, available for anything, to anyone.

11 · Built for the models that are coming

As models get stronger, understanding the person becomes the bottleneck

Raw intelligence won’t tell you that a student’s silence means “I’m scared to ask.” Every jump in capability makes that gap matter more. Five bets.

01

When thinking is almost free

Attention per person, not model size

A hundred thoughts on every sentence a learner says.

02

When models answer instantly

Thought that fits inside a pause

Real-time theory of mind becomes part of every voice agent.

03

When models learn continually

The theory becomes weights

A model that has learned you. You should own it, read it, delete it.

04

When systems improve themselves

A reward that can’t be faked

Whether a real person later does what you predicted. Growth, never engagement.

05

When intelligence is general

The last mile is one person

An AGI aligned with humanity still has to be aligned with this child, today, in this mood.
